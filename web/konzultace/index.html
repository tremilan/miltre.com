Consulting & mentoring

Strategic IT consulting and mentoring for small and mid-size companies. We help connect business goals with IT so technology moves the company forward instead of slowing it down. We reduce investment risk, help address technology debt, and make better use of your team’s capacity.

When to reach out

  • you are planning a new digital project and want to validate direction, budget, and risks before investing time and money
  • the current solution “works”, but blocks growth, is expensive to maintain, or no longer fits the company
  • you need to move your internal team forward, align ways of working, and raise seniority without expensive hiring
  • you want a second opinion on architecture, technology choices, vendors, or interim IT leadership during growth or change

What we work on together

  • Strategy and architecture: sustainable and secure solutions, technology choices, and integration of key systems (ERP, CRM, BI)
  • Audit and optimisation: analysis of infrastructure, processes, and systems, finding bottlenecks, and a clear remediation plan
  • Team mentoring: hands-on guidance for developers, architects, and IT managers, knowledge transfer, and shared standards
  • Interim IT leadership: external CTO/CIO for strategic decisions, vendor management, and oversight of the technology vision

Main benefit

Fewer blind investments, faster decisions, and a clearer direction. IT should be a tool for the business, not a project for its own sake.
