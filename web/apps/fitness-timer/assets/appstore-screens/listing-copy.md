## Description EN
Fitness Timer by Miltre is a clear interval timer for any workout you time yourself.

Create rounds and exercises, save lessons, and run work and rest intervals on your iPhone. Apple Watch shows the same session, so you can follow the timer without holding the phone.

Use it at home, in a gym, or with a group. Lessons stay on your device — no account required for normal use.

Features:
• Quick start with rounds and exercises
• Saved lessons you can reuse
• A name, duration, note, and pause for each exercise
• One exercise time or one pause applied to the whole lesson
• Clear workout view during the session, including full screen
• A sound in the last seconds of an interval
• Your own exercise database
• Apple Watch for the live session
