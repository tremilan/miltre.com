Support

Help for Fitness Timer on Mac, iPhone, Apple Watch, Android, and Windows.

Contact

Email contact@miltre.com with your platform (Mac / iPhone / Watch / Android / Windows), app version if known, and a short description of the issue.

Common tips

  • iPhone + Apple Watch: open Fitness Timer on the phone first, then on the watch. For Saved lessons on the watch, open Saved lessons on the phone while both apps are running.
  • Mac: use the desktop app from your install package. Full screen helps when projecting to a class display.
  • Android / Windows: keep the app updated from the store or your install channel; clear app storage only if you intentionally want to remove saved lessons.

Privacy

Training data stays on your device in normal use. See the Privacy Policy.
